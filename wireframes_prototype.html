Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics Key (H1 - H10)

HCI Design Reference
H1 Visibility of Status: Cutdown timers, confirmed timestamps.
H2 Match Real World: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, familiar star ratings.
H3 Control & Freedom: Edit and withdraw before deadline.
H4 Consistency: Uniform 3-choice radio interaction across app.
H5 Error Prevention: Strict automatic cutoff locks.
H6 Recognition > Recall: Menus and choices visible simultaneously.
H7 Flexibility & Efficiency: 1-click submit and 7-day tab bars.
H8 Aesthetic & Minimalist: No fake gamification or chart clutter.
H9 Error Recovery: Polite plain-language error explanations.
H10 Help & Privacy: Contextual voluntary intention notes.
